# Kundin binciken rumbun bayanai {#dataset-search-log}

Yi amfani da wannan kundin yayin da kake aiki a kan [Kafin Ka Fara](/before-you-start/). Cika kwafi ɗaya a kowane aiki (project). Ajiye shi tare da fayilolin aikinka, kuma ka raba shi da tawagarka. Yana nuna cewa ka bincika ayyukan da ake da su, kuma yana ba ka ɓangaren ayyuka masu alaƙa (related-work) na takardarka.

## Mataki na 1: Bincike {#step-1-searches}

Yi rikodin kowane bincike, har ma da waɗanda ba su samo komai ba. Binciken da bai bayar da sakamako ba ma wata kyakkyawar shaida ce mai amfani.

**Gina kalmomin bincikenka daga sassa uku**, kuma ka gwada haɗe-haɗe daban-daban:

- **Harshe:** kowane suna da lamba, misali `Hausa`, `Harshen Hausa`, `hau`, `ha`.
- **Aiki:** sunan aikin da ma'anoninsa, misali `sentiment`, `opinion`, `polarity`, `emotion`.
- **Kalmar majiya (resource word):** `dataset`, `corpus`, `benchmark`, `annotated`, `shared task`.

Teburin yana nuna misalan bincike don aikin fashin ra'ayi na Hausa. Sauya su da naka.

| | Lanfrica | misali, `Hausa`, sannan a tace (filter) ta aiki | |
| | AtlasNLP | misali, harshe: Hausa; aiki: sentiment analysis | |
| | Hugging Face (tace harshe) | misali, harshe `hau`, aiki `text-classification` | |
