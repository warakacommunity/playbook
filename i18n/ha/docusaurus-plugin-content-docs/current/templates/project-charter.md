# Samfurin kundin tsarin aiki {#project-charter-template}

*Bita ta ƙarshe: 2026-07-07.*

*Yarjejeniyar al'umma ta Mataki-na-0 wacce ta ƙunshi ƙa'idojin rubutu (orthography), yanayin amfani (use case), tsarin haƙƙin mallaka (IP framework), da tsarin tawaga wanda babi na [shigar da harsunan da ba a fiye amfani da su ba](/long-tail-language/) ya kafa hujjar cewa dole ne a amince da su a rubuce kafin a fara tattara bayanai. Kwafi shi (fork it), tattauna shi da al'umma, sanya hannu a kansa, kuma a ajiye shi tare da fitar da ma'adanar bayanai (corpus).*

## Yadda za a yi amfani da wannan samfurin {#how-to-use-this-template}

1. Kwafi duk abin da ke ƙarƙashin layin raba rubutu zuwa wani sabon fayil (`docs/project-charter.md` a cikin wurin ajiya (repo) na aikin ko wurin ajiya na al'umma).
2. Cike kowane `[BRACKETED FIELD]` da ainihin amsar da aka tattauna da al'umma, ba wanda tawagar aikin kaɗai ta yanke shawara a kai ba.
3. Kira taron tuntuɓar al'umma kamar yadda aka bayyana a [Sashi na C na samfurin amincewa](/templates/consent-form#section-c-community-consent-addendum). Kundin tsarin aikin shi ne sakamako (artefact) da ke fitowa daga wannan tuntuɓar.
4. Sanya hannu a kansa. Duk ɓangarorin da aka ambata (shugaban aiki, masu lura da al'umma, cibiyar ɗaukar nauyi, mai shiga tsakani (ombudsperson)) za su sanya hannu a kan takarda a zahiri ko ta hanyar shaida ta intanet. A ajiye kwafi ɗaya a hannun kowane ɓangare.
5. Sanya masa lamba (version). Ana iya yi wa kundin tsarin aikin kwaskwarima; yin kwaskwarima yana buƙatar irin wannan tsari na sanya hannu.

Wannan ya samo asali ne daga [shigar da harsunan da ba a fiye amfani da su ba Mataki na 0](/long-tail-language/#step-0-before-any-data-collection), babin [shari'a, amincewa, da haƙƙin mallaka na al'umma](/legal-consent/), da kuma kowane Nazarin Bincike (Case Study) a cikin littafin jagorar inda rashin kundin tsarin aiki ya zama asalin rikici a gaba (downstream dispute).

## 4. Bayanai (Data): me ake tattarawa, daga wajen wa, ta yaya {#4-data-what-is-collected-from-whom-how}

## 5. Haƙƙin mallaka (IP) na al'umma da lasisi {#5-community-ip-and-licence}

Cibiyar kundin tsarin aikin. An amsa kowace tambaya da ke ƙasa a bayyane.

- **Waye ke da mallakin ɗanyen bayanai (raw data)?** [Mai bayar da gudummawa, al'umma, aiki, tare da dalili.]
- **Waye ke da mallakin ma'adanar bayanai da aka sanya wa lakabi (annotated corpus)?** [Ɓangaren da aka ambata ko tsarin mallaka na haɗin gwiwa.]
- **Waye ke da mallakin ƙirar kwamfuta (models) da aka gina daga ma'adanar bayanan?** [Haka nan.]
- **A ƙarƙashin wane lasisi aka fitar da ma'adanar bayanan?** [Maigane na SPDX (SPDX identifier) + dalili. Duba [sashin zaɓin lasisi](/legal-consent/#licence-selection). CC BY-NC 4.0 shi ne zaɓin asali na gaskiya na littafin jagorar; a rubuta dalili idan an zaɓi wani abu daban.]
- **A ƙarƙashin wane lasisi aka fitar da ƙirar kwamfuta (models)?** [Fitar da ƙirar kwamfuta shawara ce mai zaman kanta daga fitar da bayanai; a rubuta ta.]
- **Wane sake amfani (re-use) ne ke buƙatar amincewar al'umma?** [Misali, "amfani don kasuwanci", "sanyawa a cikin fitarwa a gaba (downstream release) a ƙarƙashin wani lasisi daban", "amfani a cikin bayanan horarwa (training data) na ƙirar kwamfuta mallakar waje".]
- **Waye ke da ikon amincewa da buƙatun sake amfani?** [Kwamitin da aka ambata ko mai lura.]
- **Yaya tsarin raba riba (benefit-sharing) yake?** [Idan ma'adanar bayanan ta samar da kuɗaɗen shiga a gaba (downstream revenue), ta yaya hakan zai koma ga al'umma? Idan ba ta samarwa, a faɗi gaskiya cewa ba ta samarwa.]

- **Jadawalin lokaci:** [Kwanakin cimma buri (Milestone dates). Duba [tsarin cimma buri na 0-zuwa-4 na babin harsunan da ba a fiye amfani da su ba](/long-tail-language/#step-2-realistic-milestones) don duba misali; yi amfani da tsarin da zai yiwu a zahiri ga wannan aikin.]
- **Ƙiyasin ƙoƙari (Effort estimate):** [Watannin-mutum (Person-months) ga kowane matsayi.]

- **Tsarin biyan haƙƙi (Compensation policy):** [Adadin kuɗi ga masu sanya lakabi, masu fassara, mai tuntuɓar al'umma, mai shiga tsakani. Ana bayyana adadin kuɗin don hana matsalar (failure mode) biyan masu bayar da gudummawa daban-daban kuɗi daban-daban a kan aiki iri ɗaya.]

- **Abin da zai faru idan kasafin kuɗi ya ƙare:** [Jerin muhimmanci na abin da za a kammala da abin da za a yanke.]

- **Kula da aiki bayan an kammala:** [Waye ke da mallakin ma'adanar bayanan bayan aikin ya ƙare; waye ke karɓar buƙatun sake amfani; menene haƙƙoƙin al'umma masu dorewa.]

- **Kariyar al'adun gargajiya:** [Idan ma'adanar bayanan ta ƙunshi ilimin gargajiya ko abubuwan da suka shafi al'ada masu mahimmanci, matakan kariya a kusa da shi. Duba [tsarin ilimin gargajiya na WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/tk/en/).]
