# Tsarin Al'umma {#community-ecosystems}

Tsarin sarrafa harshe na kwamfuta na Afirka (African NLP) wani aiki ne na al'umma, ta wata hanya ta musamman. Tarin bayanai (datasets), ƙirar kwamfuta (models), da ma'aunan gwaji (benchmarks) waɗanda ke ciyar da fannin gaba, ba manyan cibiyoyi ne ke gina su ba, a'a, rukunin masu aikin sa-kai ne, ɗalibai, da masu bincike waɗanda ke aiki tare ta intanet a faɗin nahiyar. Al'ummar Masakhane ta nuna wannan ruhin a cikin sunanta, wanda ke nufin "mun gina tare" a harshen isiZulu, da kuma jaddadawarta cewa ya kamata 'yan Afirka su gina fasahar harshen Afirka, don 'yan Afirka, tare da al'umma tana mallakar abin da ta ƙirƙira ([Masakhane](/references#masakhane)). Sanin wannan tsari (ecosystem) ilimi ne mai amfani, domin a nan ne za ka sami abokan aiki, tarin bayanai, masu sanya lakabi (annotators), da kuma tallafin kuɗi.

## Al'ummomin da kuma abubuwan da suke yi {#the-communities-and-what-they-do}

Wannan tsari yana da fasali mai sauƙin ganewa. **Masakhane** ita ce cibiyar haɗin kan Afirka, wata al'umma ta asali mai ɗauke da dubban mutane wadda tsarin gudanarwarta na haɗin gwiwa ya samar da muhimman tarin bayanai kamar MasakhaNER da MAFAND ([Nekoto et al., 2020](/references#nekoto-2020); [Adelani et al., 2022](/references#adelani-2022)). A kewayenta da kuma cikinta akwai **ƙungiyoyi masu mayar da hankali kan harshe da ƙasa** kamar HausaNLP, EthioNLP, GhanaNLP, da Digital Umuganda a Rwanda, waɗanda ke da ƙwarewa sosai a kan takamaiman harsunansu. **Deep Learning Indaba**, wadda aka kafa a shekarar 2017, ita ce wurin taron al'umma na kowace shekara kuma tana gudanar da shirye-shiryen IndabaX waɗanda suka kai ƙasashe 47 nan da shekarar 2025, tana gina ƙarfin ɗan adam da fannin ya dogara da shi ([Deep Learning Indaba](/references#deep-learning-indaba)). **AI4D-Africa** ta bayar da tallafi tare da tsara ƙirƙirar tarin bayanai ta hanyar Shirinta na Harshen Afirka (African Language Program) ([Siminyu et al., 2021](/references#siminyu-2021)), **Zindi** tana ɗaukar nauyin gasanni da kuma ɗimbin masana kimiyyar bayanai (data scientists) na Afirka, **Lelapa AI** tana gina ƙirar kwamfuta da ta mayar da hankali kan Afirka da kuma tsarin Esethu, ita kuma **Lanfrica** tana tattara bayanan albarkatun don a sauƙaƙa gano su ([Lanfrica](/references#lanfrica)). Wannan ba cikakken jeri ba ne, kuma yana ƙaruwa a kowace shekara.

## Haɗin gwiwar masana da masana'antu {#academic-and-industry-collaboration}

Kyakkyawan tarin bayanai na harsunan Afirka yana ƙara fitowa ne daga haɗin gwiwar da ya haɗa masana, masana'antu, da al'umma. Cibiyoyin jami'o'i kamar ƙungiyar Data Science for Social Impact a Jami'ar Pretoria, Maseno Centre for Applied AI a Kenya, da SADiLaR, wato cibiyar albarkatun harshe ta ƙasar Afirka ta Kudu, suna kawo zurfin ilimin harshe da dorewa. Kamfanoni da masu bayar da tallafi suna kawo faɗaɗawa da kuɗi: African Next Voices ta haɗa cibiyoyin al'umma da tallafin Gidauniyar Gates (Gates Foundation) don ɗaukar muryoyin magana na dubban sa'o'i ([African Next Voices, 2025](/references#african-next-voices)), ita kuma Lacuna Fund tana ɗaukar nauyin ƙirƙirar tarin bayanai a faɗin nahiyar ([Lacuna Fund, n.d.](/references#lacuna-fund)). Mafi inganci daga cikin waɗannan haɗin gwiwar suna barin al'umma ta ci gaba da sarrafa bayanan koda kuwa kuɗin daga waje ya fito, wanda shi ne ainihin abin da babin [kula da bayanai (data governance)](/data-governance/) yake fafutukar gani.

## Bayar da gudummawa, da kuma karrama masu bayar da gudummawa {#contributing-and-crediting-contributors}

Albarkatun al'umma suna yin ƙarfi ne kawai daidai da mutanen da ke bayar da gudummawa a cikinsu, don haka ka sauƙaƙa bayar da gudummawa kuma ka tabbatar da an karrama mutane a aikace. Ka samar da bayyanannun ƙa'idojin bayar da gudummawa waɗanda ke faɗin irin taimakon da ake buƙata, ko rubutu ne, dubawa, raba tarin bayanai, ko fassara, da kuma yadda za a fara. Ka rage wahalar bayar da gudummawa ta farko, domin da yawa daga cikin mutane masu matuƙar muhimmanci a fannin sun fara ne a matsayin ɗalibai waɗanda suka gyara wani ƙaramin abu guda ɗaya. Kuma ka ambaci sunayen masu bayar da gudummawa a cikin takardun bayanai (datasheets), a cikin wallafe-wallafe, da kuma a cikin bayanan fitarwa (release notes), domin karramawa adalci ne kuma ita ce hanya mafi sauƙi ta ci gaba da rayar da al'ummar masu aikin sa-kai. Ci gaban fannin sarrafa harshe na kwamfuta na Afirka (African NLP) a cikin shekaru ashirin, daga 'yan takardu kaɗan a shekara zuwa ɗaruruwa, ya faru ne ainihin sakamakon wannan faɗaɗawar rukunin masu bayar da gudummawa ([Belay et al., 2025](/references#belay-2025)).
