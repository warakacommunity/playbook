# Haɗin Gwiwa da Ayyukan Tarayya {#collaboration-and-shared-tasks}

Baya ga tsayayyun al'ummomi, NLP na Afirka yana samun ci gaba ta hanyar tsare-tsaren haɗin gwiwa: ayyukan tarayya (shared tasks), ma'aunan aiki (benchmarks), bita (workshops), da kuma buɗaɗɗun ƙalubale (open challenges) waɗanda ke tattara ƙoƙari zuwa ga wata manufa ɗaya. A fagen da babu wata ƙungiya guda ɗaya da za ta iya ɗaukar ɗaruruwan harsuna, waɗannan su ne hanyoyin da ke mayar da ayyukan da suka watsu zuwa gundarin aikin haɗin gwiwa (shared infrastructure).

## Ayyukan tarayya da ma'aunan aiki {#shared-tasks-and-benchmarks}

Aikin tarayya yana tsara matsala ɗaya, saitin bayanai (dataset) ɗaya, da kuma hanyar bayar da maki ɗaya, sannan ya gayyaci kowa don shiga ciki. Wannan yana ninka ƙoƙari: ƙungiyoyi da yawa suna gina bayanai da ƙira (models) don manufa ɗaya a lokaci guda, kuma fagen yana samun ma'aunin aiki don auna ci gaba a nan gaba. NLP na Afirka ya yi amfani da wannan tsari akai-akai kuma yadda ya kamata. AfriSenti ya zama aikin tarayya na SemEval wanda ya samar da saitin bayanan ra'ayi (sentiment datasets) na harsuna goma sha huɗu kuma ya jawo tsarin mahalarta da yawa ([Muhammad et al., 2023](/references#muhammad-2023)), ƙoƙarin MasakhaNER ya tsara lakabi na sunayen abubuwa (named-entity annotation) a cikin harsuna ashirin ([Adelani et al., 2022](/references#adelani-2022)), kuma ma'aunan aiki kamar IrokoBench yanzu suna ba al'umma ma'aunin haɗin gwiwa don manyan ƙirar harshe (large language models) a kan harsunan Afirka. Ba da gudummawa ga aikin tarayya na ɗaya daga cikin hanyoyin da suka fi dacewa don ƙara sabon harshe a taswira, saboda za ku gaji tsarin aiki (protocol), kayan aiki (tooling), da kuma kasancewa tare da wasu da ke yin aiki iri ɗaya.

## Bita da buɗaɗɗun ƙalubale {#workshops-and-open-challenges}

Bita da ƙalubale su ne wuraren da al'umma ke taruwa, tsarawa, da kuma ɗaukar sababbin mambobi. Bitar AfricaNLP, wanda ake gudanarwa kowace shekara tare da manyan tarurruka, shi ne babban wurin fagen don sabbin ayyukan harsunan Afirka, kuma Deep Learning Indaba da abubuwan da ke faruwa na IndabaX suna gina ƙwarewa da alaƙar da ke sa haɗin gwiwa ya yiwu tun da farko ([Deep Learning Indaba](/references#deep-learning-indaba)). Buɗaɗɗun ƙalubale suna karkatar da wannan kuzari zuwa ga bayanai kai tsaye. Gasar Zindi da AI4D African Language Dataset Challenge sun ƙarfafa ƙirƙirar buɗaɗɗun saitin bayanai waɗanda ba su wanzu a baya ba ([Siminyu et al., 2021](/references#siminyu-2021)), kuma kiran Deep Learning Indaba na 2026 don saitin bayanan Afirka ya ci gaba da tsarin a fannin rubutu, magana, da hangen nesa (vision). Idan kuna farawa, shiga ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan galibi ya fi dacewa a matsayin matakin farko fiye da yin aiki kaɗai, saboda al'umma za ta taimaka muku gina wani abu mai ɗorewa.
