# Yadda za a karanta wannan littafin jagora {#how-to-read-this-playbook}

Wannan littafin jagora yana bin tsarin gina rumbun bayanai (dataset) tun daga farko har zuwa ƙarshe. Taswirar da ke ƙasa ta kasa babi-babinsa zuwa matakai huɗu na gina rumbun bayanai. Zaɓi kowane babi don tafiya kai tsaye zuwa gare shi.

<ChapterMap />

Ba lallai sai ka karanta shi a jere ba. Zaɓi hanyar da ta dace da inda kake:

- **Kai sabon shiga ne a harkar gina rumbun bayanai.** Karanta [Gabatarwa](/introduction), sannan ka bi babi-babin Tushe (Foundations) a jere: [Gudanar da Aiki](/project-management/), [Tattara Bayanai](/data-collection/Overview), [Kula da Bayanai](/data-governance/), [Tsarin Sanya Lakabi (Annotation Design)](/annotation-design/annotation-task-design) da [Ingancin Bayanai](/data-quality/). Kowane babi yana ginuwa ne a kan wanda ya gabace shi.
- **Ka san aikin da za ka yi kuma kana so ka guji maimaita aiki.** Fara da [Kafin Ka Fara](/before-you-start/). Yana nuna maka yadda za ka nemo rumbun bayanai da ake da su, ka auna ko sun dace, kuma ka yanke shawara ko za ka sake amfani da su, ka faɗaɗa su, ko ka gina naka.
- **Tuni kana da ɗanyen bayanai (raw data) kuma kana buƙatar a sanya musu lakabi (annotated).** Tafi zuwa [Tsarin Sanya Lakabi](/annotation-design/annotation-task-design), sannan [Ingancin Bayanai](/data-quality/).
- **Kana aiki da magana (speech), hotuna, bidiyo ko takardu.** Tafi zuwa ɓangaren da ya dace da yanayin bayananka (modality): [Magana](/sections/speech), [Gani (Vision)](/sections/vision) ko [Nau'o'i Daban-daban (Multimodal)](/sections/multimodal). Kowane yana bayanin abin da ke canzawa da zarar ka wuce rubutu zalla.
- **Harshenka yana da ƙarancin rubutun na'ura (digital text) ko ma ba shi da shi gaba ɗaya.** Karanta [Shigar da harshe mai ƙarancin bayanai (Long-tail language onboarding)](/long-tail-language/) kafin komai. Yana ƙunshi tsarin rubutu (orthography), al'umma da kuma shawarwarin mallaka waɗanda ke zuwa da farko.
- **Kana amfani da LLMs don ƙirƙira ko ƙara yawan bayanai.** Karanta [Bayanai Taimakon LLM (LLM-Assisted Data)](/llm-assisted-task/) don sanin fa'idodi da matakan kariya.
- **Kana shirya rumbun bayanai don sakewa (release).** Karanta [Rubutattun Bayanai (Documentation)](/documentation/documentation), [Tsawon Rayuwar Rumbun Bayanai (Dataset Lifecycle)](/dataset-lifecycle/release) da [Shari'a, amincewa, da haƙƙin mallakar fasaha na al'umma (Legal, consent, and community IP)](/legal-consent/).
- **Ba ka kan intanet ko kuma yanar gizonka tana da nauyi.** Sauke dukkan littafin jagorar a matsayin PDF guda ɗaya daga menu na **AfriPlaybook** da ke kan mashigin kewayawa (navbar). Ana sake gina shi a duk lokacin da aka fitar da sabon tsari.
- **Gara ka karanta a wani harshe daban.** Yi amfani da maɓallin canza harshe da ke sama a hannun dama. Al'umma ne ke kula da fassarorin kuma suna ƙaruwa a tsawon lokaci.

A cikin littafin gaba ɗaya za ka sami samfura (templates) waɗanda za ka iya daidaitawa da buƙatarka, kamar fom ɗin amincewa, ƙa'idojin sanya lakabi (annotation guidelines) da jerin abubuwan duba shugabanci (governance checklists), tare da misalan da aka yi aiki a kansu daga ainihin ayyukan harsunan Afirka da kuma hanyoyin haɗi (links) zuwa rumbun bayanai da kayan aikin da za ka iya sake amfani da su. An fassara sababbin kalmomi a cikin [ƙamus (glossary)](/glossary).
