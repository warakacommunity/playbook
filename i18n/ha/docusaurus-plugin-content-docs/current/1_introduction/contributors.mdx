# Masu ba da gudummawarmu {#our-contributors}

'Yan sa-kai ne suka rubuta kuma suke kula da AfriPlaybook: masu bincike, ɗalibai, masana kimiyyar harshe (linguists), masu fassara, masu sanya lakabi (annotators), da masu tsara al'umma (community organisers). Mun gode wa duk wanda ya taimaka wajen gina shi. A ƙasa akwai mutanen da suka ba da gudummawa ga [ma'adanar playbook (playbook repository)](https://github.com/warakacommunity/playbook). Ana sabunta jerin sunayen duk lokacin da aka wallafa shafin.

<ContributorGrid />

## Ba a jera ka a nan ba? {#not-listed-here}

Yawancin aikin da ke bayan jagora irin wannan ba ya bayyana a GitHub: duba babi-babi, fassara shafuka, sanya lakabi ga bayanai (annotating data), da kuma tsara mutane. Idan ka taimaka ta ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan hanyoyin, buɗe [matsala (issue) a GitHub](https://github.com/warakacommunity/playbook/issues/new) ko ka gaya mana a [Discord](https://discord.gg/ChNPHV2PPS) don mu ba ka yabo.

Idan ka tura aiki (committed) zuwa ga ma'adanar amma ba ka cikin jerin sunayen, duba don tabbatar da cewa adireshin imel ɗin da kake tura aiki da shi yana haɗe da asusunka na GitHub.

Don shiga jerin masu ba da gudummawa, duba [Yadda za a ba da gudummawa](/introduction/how-to-contribute).
