# Tsare-tsaren Kuɗi da Albarkatu {#cost-and-resource-planning}

Koyi yadda za a tsara albarkatun da ake buƙata yadda ya kamata don ƙirƙirar rumbun bayanai (dataset), gami da tsara kasafin kuɗi, jadawalin lokaci, da dabarun faɗaɗawa.

## Dalilin da ya sa Tsare-tsare ke da Muhimmanci {#why-planning-matters}

Ƙirƙirar rumbun bayanai na iya cin albarkatu sosai. Kyakkyawan tsari yana taimakawa wajen tabbatar da ingantaccen amfani da lokaci, kasafin kuɗi, da ƙoƙarin ɗan adam yayin da ake kiyaye ingancin bayanai.

## Muhimman Abubuwan Tsare-tsare {#key-components-of-planning}

### Tsara Kasafin Kuɗin Lakabi (Annotation) {#budgeting-annotation-costs}

- **Ƙiyasin kuɗin lakabi** – Lissafa kuɗin kowane samfuri ko kowane aiki  
- **Tsara ma'aikata** – Yi la'akari da ƙwararru idan aka kwatanta da masu yin lakabi na jama'a (crowd annotators)  
- **Kuɗin kayan aiki** – Haɗa da dandamali, wurin ajiya, da ababen more rayuwa  
- **Kuɗin kula da inganci** – Yi lissafin matakan tantancewa da dubawa  

### Ƙiyasin Lokaci {#time-estimation}

- **Sarƙaƙiyar aiki** – Ayyuka masu sarƙaƙiya suna buƙatar ƙarin lokaci ga kowane lakabi  
- **Gudun mai yin lakabi** – Yi ƙiyasi bisa ga gwaje-gwajen farko ko ma'auni  
- **Matakan aiki** – Haɗa da shiryawa, bayar da horo, yin lakabi, da tantancewa  
- **Ƙarin lokaci** – Yi tsari don jinkiri da maimaitawa  

### Dabarun Faɗaɗawa {#scaling-strategy}

- **Faɗaɗawa kaɗan-kaɗan** – Fara da ƙanana sannan a faɗaɗa a hankali  
- **Taimakon na'ura mai sarrafa kanta (automation)** – Yi amfani da kayan aiki don gaggauta tsara bayanai na farko da tantancewa  
- **Gudanar da ayyuka a lokaci ɗaya** – Raba ayyuka ga masu yin lakabi da yawa  
- **Daidaita inganci da faɗaɗawa** – Kiyaye ingancin bayanai yayin da ake ƙara girmansu
