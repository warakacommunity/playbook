# Da'a, Karkata (Bias), da Gudanarwa {#ethics-bias-and-governance}

Koyi yadda za a tabbatar da ƙirƙirar rumbun bayanai (dataset) cikin alhaki ta hanyar magance karkata (bias), kare sirri, da kiyaye gaskiya a duk tsawon rayuwar bayanan (data lifecycle).

## Dalilin da ya sa Da'a da Gudanarwa Suke da Muhimmanci {#why-ethics-and-governance-matter}

Rumbun bayanai (datasets) suna shafar yanayin aikin tsarin fasahar zamani na harshe (language AI systems) kai tsaye. Rashin kyakkyawan kulawa da karkata, sirri, ko abubuwa masu mahimmanci (sensitive content) na iya haifar da sakamako mai cutarwa. Ayyukan da'a da tsare-tsaren gudanarwa (governance frameworks) suna taimakawa wajen tabbatar da adalci, riƙon amana, da yarda.

## Muhimman Abubuwan Ayyukan Bayanai na Da'a {#key-components-of-ethical-data-practices}

### Gano Karkata da Rage ta {#bias-identification-and-mitigation}

- **Gano karkata (Bias detection)** – Gano rashin daidaito ko wakilci mai karkata a cikin bayanai  
- **Karkatar majiya (Source bias)** – Auna karkatar da majiyoyin bayanai suka kawo  
- **Karkatar lakabi (Annotation bias)** – Kula da rashin jituwa a tsakanin masu saka lakabi (annotators)  
- **Dabarun rage karkata (Mitigation strategies)** – Aiwatar da sake ɗaukar samfuri (re-sampling), sake auna nauyi (re-weighting), ko tace ƙa'idoji  

### Gano da Cire Bayanan Sirri na Mutum (PII) {#pii-detection-and-removal}

- **Gano bayanan mutum** – Gano sunaye, adireshi, bayanan tuntuɓa, da abubuwan gane mutum (identifiers)  
- **Kayan aikin gano abubuwa masu sarrafa kansu** – Yi amfani da ƙira (models) ko ƙa'idoji don nuna bayanan sirri  
- **Bita da hannu (Manual review)** – Tabbatar da abin da na'ura ta gano ta hanyar binciken ɗan adam  
- **Cire bayanai ko ɓoye su** – Goge ko ɓoye abubuwan gane mutum  

### Dabarun Ɓoye Suna (Anonymization) {#anonymization-strategies}

- **Cire shaidar gane mutum (De-identification)** – Cire ko canza bayanan da za a iya gane mutum da su  
- **Amfani da sunan bogi (Pseudonymization)** – Canza abubuwan gane mutum da lakabi na wucin gadi  
- **Haɗawa wuri guda (Aggregation)** – Gabatar da bayanai a matsayin rukuni don hana sake gane mutum  
- **Auna haɗari (Risk assessment)** – Auna haɗarin sake gane mutum bayan an ɓoye suna  

### Kula da Siffofi da Abubuwa Masu Mahimmanci (Sensitive Attributes) {#sensitive-attribute-and-content-handling}

- **Siffofi masu mahimmanci** – Jinsi, ƙabila, addini, lafiya, ko ra'ayoyin siyasa  
- **Tace abubuwan da ke ciki (Content moderation)** – Kula da abubuwa masu cutarwa, masu ɓata rai, ko na batsa a tsanake  
- **Kula da shiga (Access control)** – Takaita bayanan sirri ga masu amfani da aka ba wa izini kawai  
- **Daidaita yanayin amfani (Use-case alignment)** – Yanke shawarar sakawa a ciki bisa ga buƙatun aikin  

### Wakilci na Adalci {#fair-representation}

- **Ɗaukar samfuri mai haɗawa (Inclusive sampling)** – Tabbatar da wakilci daban-daban a tsakanin ƙungiyoyi  
- **Rumbun bayanai masu daidaito** – Guji wakilci da ya wuce kima ko wanda bai taka kara ya karya ba  
- **Sanin yanayi (Context awareness)** – Yi la'akari da bambancin al'adu da harshe  
- **Adalcin kimantawa (Evaluation fairness)** – Gwada ƙira (models) a tsakanin ƙananan ƙungiyoyi daban-daban  

### Rubuta Haɗari da Gaskiya {#risk-documentation-and-transparency}

- **Gano haɗari** – Rubuta yiwuwar cutarwa da gazawa  
- **Takardun bayanai (Datasheets) da rubutawa** – Bayar da bayyanannun bayanai game da rumbun bayanai  
- **Ayyukan gaskiya** – Raba bayanan tattarawa, sarrafawa, da saka lakabi (annotation)  
- **Manufofin gudanarwa** – Ƙayyade ƙa'idoji don amfani da rarraba rumbun bayanai
